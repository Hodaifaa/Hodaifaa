# Surface: the game (kazzab/site)

Scope: the whole pass-the-phone game, one screen at a time. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: friends at a sahra passing one phone; each screen must say whose turn it is and be safe to hand over.
Constraints: Arabic RTL, Jordanian dialect, works offline, no mascot, must not read as a kids' game or a generic dark app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The game is played on the coffeehouse table. It refuses the party-app arrangement of rounded cards, emoji tiles and a cartoon mascot on a purple-black ground.

OWN-WORLD: Painted qahwa wall bottle green as the ground, bone for text, mustard for the one primary action, oxblood for accusation and the liar. Ruq'a lettering (Aref Ruqaa) for names and words, Almarai for everything worked with. Square-shouldered controls, dotted menu leaders, hairline rules instead of cards. Cement-tile pattern only where something is face down.

STORY: The player knows at a glance whose turn it is, sees a word or learns they are the liar, argues, votes, and wants another round.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home. Wordmark in ruq'a at top right, players as a row of checkers, categories as a café menu list with dotted leaders and counts, round settings, and one mustard button pinned to the bottom.

FORM: The qahwa table, candidate 5 of 7. Signature move: players are backgammon checkers and votes stack as checkers on a board point. seed:c5ccd5d9621f8fb0
